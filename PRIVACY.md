# Roomcalc privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Roomcalc does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Rooms, measurements and prices you enter are saved only on your device.
- Room scanning uses Apple RoomPlan with the camera and LiDAR sensor. The scan is processed on the device; no images, video or 3-D data are recorded or sent anywhere. Only the measured sizes (walls, floor area, doors, windows) are kept.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The app does not need an internet connection.
- When you share a shopping list, it goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/roomcalc/issues
